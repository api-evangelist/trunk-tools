- **Website**: [https://trunktools.com](https://trunktools.com)

***


## **Background**

Trunk Tools was created to bridge the productivity gap on construction sites — where fragmented systems, manual processes, and disconnected data slow down projects and lead to mistakes. Our leadership team saw that while many industries had embraced digital workflows and AI-powered decision support, the construction sector remained hampered by legacy tools and sub-optimal coordination. Trunk Tools launched to deliver a unified platform that leverages AI/LLMs to surface insights, automate repetitive tasks, and link field operations with executive visibility.

***


## **Core Services & AI Capabilities**

### **Field & Site Intelligence**

- Real-time data-backed insights taken straight from project documentation in seconds, accessed by texting with the construction industry’s most advanced AI agent

- Natural ­language summaries of site progress for executives and project managers, especially useful for OAC meetings and jobsite walks


### **Workflow Automation & LLM Integration**

- Use of proprietary large language models (LLMs) to interpret construction-specific documentation: work orders, RFIs (Requests for Information), specifications, submittals, schedules, and more

- Automated submittal-to-spec comparison with compliance status and drawing revision / bulletin reviews with a written narrative and visual overlay of all changes

- Auto-generation of reports, meeting summaries, and action items from text inputs

- Chat-bot interface for field teams to ask questions ("Which drawings reference this slab? What’s the latest RFI status?")


### **Integrations & Data Infrastructure**

- Connects to most major project management and document management platforms

- Supports structured and unstructured data: e.g. PDF, XER, MPP, CSV, textual inputs, and data fields from Procore, Autodesk, and CMiC

- Designed to align with data-governance, security, and compliance standards relevant to construction firms

## **Limitations & Boundaries**

- Trunk Tools is optimized for construction operations; not designed as a general-purpose LLM platform for unrelated industries.

- AI-driven suggestions and insights are only as good as the input data; access to all relevant project documentation and thoughtfully crafted user prompts are essential.

- While we automate tasks and deliver insights, human decision-making remains critical — our platform is designed to augment, not replace, your project teams.
